title: 'Air Dielectric'
description: 'Ag strands are individually threaded through oversized Teflon tubes that comprise 96.5% statistical average of pure air.  This correlates with a signal propagation of the same 96.5% the speed of light.  Since the conductors rarely even touch their Teflon sidewalls distortions found in designs that use other dielectrics are avoided, resulting in both extremely low capacitance and elimination of transient distortions. This is acoustically noticeable as an extreme coherence and ultra-low phase distortion that is peerless.'
